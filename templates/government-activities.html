Recurring National Health Programs
Free government initiatives conducted periodically

Pulse Polio Programme
National Immunization Day (NID)
Free oral polio drops for every child aged 0–5 years. Booths may be available at Anganwadi centres, schools, railway stations and other designated locations.
Ages 0–5 100% Free No registration Immunization booth
🩹
Mission Indradhanush
Catch-up Immunization Drive
Special vaccination rounds for children and pregnant women who may have missed routine immunization doses.
Children Pregnant women Catch-up doses Free
🪱
National Deworming Day
School & Anganwadi Deworming
Free deworming medicine is provided to children and adolescents through schools and Anganwadi centres as part of the national deworming program.
Ages 1–19 Free medicine Schools Anganwadi
🩺
Ayushman Bharat Health Camps
Free Screening & Awareness
Periodic health activities may include screening for blood pressure, diabetes and selected common health conditions at public health facilities.
BP screening Diabetes Health check Free
