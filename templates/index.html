→ Why We Built PR Harvest

Bringing Primary Care Guidance to Every Village

Rural and tier-2 populations often face long travel times, limited primary healthcare access, language barriers, and delayed medical guidance. PR Harvest gives people a preliminary, AI-assisted symptom check so they know whether their situation truly needs urgent professional attention.

How It Works

Three Steps to Clarity, No Clinic Visit Required

01

Describe Your Symptoms

Type or speak what you're feeling, in English, Hindi, or Marathi — no medical terms needed.

02

AI Assesses Urgency

03

Get a Clear Next Step
