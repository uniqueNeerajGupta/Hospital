NEXORA 2026 · Problem Statement PR•HARVEST

Healthcare guidance shouldn't depend on where you live.

PR Harvest is an AI-assisted preliminary triage platform built for rural and tier-2 India — helping people know whether to rest, visit a clinic, or seek emergency care, in their own language, without a hospital visit just to find out.

The Problem

Access shouldn't decide your health outcome

Over 65% of India's population lives in rural and tier-2 areas, where the nearest specialist can be hours away, language barriers make medical guidance hard to understand, and people either ignore serious symptoms or make unnecessary long trips for minor ones.

What We Built

One platform, the whole journey

From the first symptom to a booked hospital bed — PR Harvest covers the full path a rural family takes when someone falls sick.

🩺

Symptom Triage

🚨

One-Tap SOS
